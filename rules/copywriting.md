# Copywriting Rules

Applies to every user-facing string in the app.

## No em dashes

- **Never use an em dash (`—`) in user-facing copy.** It reads as an AI tell.
- Replace it with the punctuation the sentence actually needs:
  - Label → value: use a colon. `Logged: 3,800 kcal`.
  - Two clauses / an aside: a comma, a period, or two sentences.
  - Separating equal fragments in a compact line: a middot `·` (`3 sets · 12 reps`).
- The en dash (`–`) is only allowed inside a numeric range (`1400–1470`, `Mon–Fri`).

## Voice

- Short, concrete, human. Buttons are verbs (`Start workout`, `Save`), not descriptions.
- Sentence case for titles, labels, and buttons (`Add exercise`, not `Add Exercise`), matching the platform voice.
- Empty states say what to do next (`No workouts yet. Start your first one below.`), never a bare `No data`.
- Confirm dialogs name the thing (`Delete "Leg day"?`) and the destructive button repeats the verb (`Delete`), never `OK`/`Yes`.
- No filler (`Please note that…`, `Successfully…`) and no exclamation marks outside a genuine celebration.
